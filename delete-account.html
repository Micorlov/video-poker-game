Delete your Royal Video Poker account

This page is for the Android app "Video Poker" (com.micorlov.videopoker). Having an account is optional: it is only created if you sign in with Google to use the Friends tab.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open the app and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account in the Account section and confirm.

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app, send an email to micorlov@gmail.com from the address of the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Video Poker account". We will delete the account and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (display name, photo, invite code and daily score).
  • Your friend connections, on both sides.
  • Your sign-in (the Firebase Authentication account).

What is kept

  • Chips and progress stored on your device. They are never uploaded; uninstalling the app removes them.
  • Invite-reward records (account identifier, display name and reward status), kept so a reward cannot be claimed twice.
  • Anonymous usage analytics held by Google Analytics, which are not linked to your account.

See the privacy policy for details.
