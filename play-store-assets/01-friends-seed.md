## English (WhatsApp / Messenger)

Hey! I built a video poker game for Android — completely free, no ads, no real money.
Would mean a lot if you install it, play ~10 hands, and leave a 5-star rating with one sentence. New apps live or die on those first ratings.

https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.micorlov.videopoker

What helps most:
1. Install and actually play ~10 hands (don't install and uninstall)
2. Rate 5 stars
3. Write one sentence mentioning "video poker" or "no ads" — Google indexes review text

Thanks!

## Rules that matter
- Spread the installs over several days, not all in one hour. A burst of installs from one place looks unnatural.
- Do NOT offer anything in exchange for a rating. That violates Play policy and can get reviews wiped.
- Ask people to write their own sentence. Identical reviews get filtered.
