Welcome to Video Poker
Classic Jacks or Better poker, a daily ALL IN bet, and leaderboards you can share with friends.
🃏
How to Play
  • Bet — pick a stake, then tap Deal.
  • Hold — tap the cards you want to keep.
  • Draw — tap Deal again to replace the rest.
  • Win — a pair of Jacks or better pays out; bigger hands pay more. The full Payout Table is always in Settings.
🏆
More Than Solo Play
  • Friends & Rooms — add friends and create poker rooms to compare scores.
  • Leaderboards & Champions — climb the hourly and daily boards.
  • ALL IN — once a day, bet your whole balance for a shot at a big win.
