Privacy Policy for Royal Video Poker

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Royal Video Poker ("we", "us") is a free social casino game published on Google Play as "Video Poker" (com.micorlov.videopoker). This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

  • Gameplay data stored on your device: your chip balance, hand statistics, settings and progress are saved on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers and are not backed up by us.
  • Account information (only if you sign in): signing in is optional and is only needed for the Friends tab. If you sign in with Google, we receive your display name, email address, profile photo and a unique account identifier.
  • Friends and invites (only if you sign in): your invite code, the connections between you and the friends you add, today's net chip profit (shown to your friends on the daily leaderboard), and records of invite rewards.
  • Invite links: if you install the app from a friend's invite link, Google Play passes us the invite code in the install referrer so we can connect you with that friend.
  • Analytics and device identifiers: we use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used. It collects app events (for example, app opened, onboarding step, hand played, daily bonus claimed), device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version), an app instance identifier, your Android advertising ID, and an approximate location (country or city) derived from your IP address.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, the app schedules a daily reminder on your device. We do not collect a push notification token.

Advertising ID

The app has access to your Android advertising ID. We use it only for analytics through Google Analytics for Firebase. We do not use it to show ads, and we do not share it with advertisers. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

What We Don't Collect

  • We do not process real-money payments or financial account information.
  • We do not collect precise location data.
  • We do not serve third-party advertising.

How We Use Data

Data is used to operate the game's features (the Friends tab, invites and invite rewards), to understand and improve how the app is used, and to prevent invite rewards from being claimed twice.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore and Analytics) as our service provider; your data is processed by Google on our behalf. If you sign in, your friends can see your display name and today's net chip profit.

Data Deletion

If you signed in, you can delete your account in the app: open Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, your friend connections and your daily score from our servers, and signs you out. Chips and progress stored on your device are not affected; uninstalling the app removes them.

You can also request deletion without the app on our account deletion page, or by contacting micorlov@gmail.com.

Invite-reward records (the account identifier, display name and reward status) are kept after deletion so that a reward cannot be claimed twice. Analytics data is retained by Google Analytics according to its retention settings and is not linked to your account.

No Real Money Gambling

This game is intended for entertainment purposes only. It does not offer real money gambling or the ability to win real money or prizes.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children and is not intended for users under 13 (or the applicable local age of digital consent).

Changes to This Policy
